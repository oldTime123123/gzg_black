## Design Context

### Users
- Primary users are assumed to be institutional investors, high-value trading clients, and advanced retail users who access the product from mobile devices.
- Their context is time-sensitive market review, account management, capital transfer, and trade execution in a compact mobile-first workflow.
- Their main jobs are:
  - Review market signals, news, and product opportunities quickly.
  - Execute trading, funding, loan, and account actions with high confidence.
  - Manage records, verification, and support access without losing trust in the platform.

### Brand Personality
- Working assumption: calm, credible, disciplined.
- The interface should feel secure, premium, and operational rather than playful or promotional.
- Emotional goals:
  - Increase confidence during sensitive financial actions.
  - Reduce noise and hesitation in transaction-heavy flows.
  - Communicate control, structure, and trustworthiness.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Mobile-first premium finance product with a restrained dark theme, warm amber emphasis, dense information hierarchy, and low-noise surfaces.
- Existing references inferred from the implementation:
  - Professional trading dashboards
  - Editorial finance layouts
  - Secure account-management flows
- Anti-reference assumptions:
  - Should not look like generic AI landing pages.
  - Should not feel playful, crypto-neon, or marketing-heavy.
  - Should avoid excessive glassmorphism, decorative gradients, and repeated “hero metric card” templates.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize trust over novelty.
   Every screen should feel stable, deliberate, and safe for financial actions.
2. Keep information hierarchy sharp.
   Prices, balances, status, and primary actions must dominate; decoration stays secondary.
3. Design for fast mobile execution.
   Touch targets, spacing, and reading flow should support one-handed scanning and low-friction action.
4. Use amber as a signal, not wallpaper.
   Brand color should guide focus and emphasis, not flood the interface.
5. Reduce visual noise while preserving premium feel.
   Favor strong typography, consistent surfaces, and clean grouping over stacked cards and ornamental effects.

## Assumptions

- This context is synthesized from the current codebase because explicit product and brand guidance was not yet provided.
- Current working quality bar assumption: flagship-level polish for key user-facing flows, with production-grade consistency across `pages` and `components`.
